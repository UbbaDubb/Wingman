Two model limitations, documented and worked around

1. Upside-wing moneyness artifact

The unshifted 2-component mixture assumes both components share one forward, producing a near-symmetric smile — it cannot reproduce SPY's monotonic downside skew, so it systematically underprices the upside wing at a roughly constant moneyness offset (~1.4–2%). Confirmed against Brigo's own MDD lecture notes, which describe a shifted extension as the correct fix. Contained, not fixed, via a tradable-moneyness band and per-leg/aggregate exposure caps.

2. Mixture degeneracy under a persistent regime

When the smile sits somewhere the two-component fit handles badly, the optimizer can satisfy the residual by collapsing lam toward 1 and letting the now near-weightless second component's sigma2 drift arbitrarily — a calibration artifact, not a real vol regime. The degenerate-fit guard (plausibility band [0.05, 1.0]) catches every instance before it reaches a trade.

What drove the loss (approximate attribution — not cleanly separable without position-level P&L)

Monday's K=777 straddle cluster (9 duplicate fills): Placed near ATM at approximately $10–12 debit each when SPY was ~769. As of Thursday close (SPY 773), put intrinsic is $777 − $773 = $4, with ~2 weeks of time value remaining — a significant reduction from the peak $12–15 put intrinsic reached Tuesday (SPY 762). Theta decay is the dominant cost: 9 contracts × ~$100 multiplier × net decay since entry. This cluster is the largest single contributor to the unrealized drawdown.

Short call verticals (the two different-signal trades): With SPY recovering from ~762 to ~773 across Wed–Thu, OTM call verticals in the 754/755 and 769/770-adjacent strikes are net losers as spot moved toward or past the short leg. Rough magnitude: 2 structures, limited by the spread width, so capped loss contribution.

General theta decay: With 25 open legs and 15 calendar days to expiry, theta is uniformly negative across the book. Daily decay for ATM straddles accelerates sharply in the final 3–4 weeks; this is a constant cost that compounds across the days the book sat open without a significant directional move.

This decomposition is approximate. Clean attribution requires position-level cost-basis and current MTM, which requires the account API. The proportions above are consistent with the known structure of the book and the SPY price path; they should not be quoted as exact dollar amounts in the write-up.
